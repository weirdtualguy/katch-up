KCC721 proposal: Kaspa NFTs with covenant rules

Aug 23, 2026 11:12 UTC 1 min read

What Happened

Developers have released an early draft for "KCC721," a new way to handle NFTs on Kaspa that keeps them native to the network and allows users to send multiple NFTs in a single transaction. A community member also asked if this batch-transfer feature could be applied to Kaspa Name Service (KNS) domains.

Why It Matters

This matters because it could make managing and trading multiple Kaspa digital collectibles much faster and cheaper, with a working test version already live for the community to try out and discuss.
