KCC-02: Control Principal References in Program ABIs

Aug 23, 2026 11:13 UTC 1 min read

What Happened

The discussion focuses on how smart contracts should clearly define who "owns" or controls a digital token or asset, whether that owner is an individual person or another smart contract program. Participants suggested creating a standard way to list these owners so that crypto wallets can easily scan the blockchain and show users the assets they actually control. The group agreed that keeping different types of identifiers separate is important, and that wallets should always double-check the on-chain data to ensure security before confirming ownership to the user.
