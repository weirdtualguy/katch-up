Kaspa Calls for Conventions (new KCC repo)

Aug 23, 2026 11:13 UTC 1 min read

What Happened

The Kaspa community has launched a new online space called "Kaspa Calls for Conventions" (KCC) to help different developers and apps work better together. Think of it as a shared idea board where people can agree on common standards for things like digital wallets and tools, without changing the core foundation of Kaspa. This matters because it makes it much easier for different apps and wallets to speak the same language and seamlessly connect with one another.
