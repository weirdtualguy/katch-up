Exploring a trust-minimized KRC-20 to KCC-20 bridge on Kaspa without relying on a relayer

Aug 23, 2026 11:12 UTC 1 min read

What Happened

Crypto builders were discussing how to securely move tokens between different parts of the Kaspa network without having to trust a middleman. Someone suggested using smart contract "covenants," but the main roadblock is that Kaspa's base network cannot currently read the off-chain ledger records needed to verify token balances.

Why It Matters

Ultimately, the discussion highlights that a fully trust-free bridge is currently blocked because the necessary proof systems (like ZK proofs or state checkpoints) aren't yet available on Kaspa to verify token transactions on-chain.
