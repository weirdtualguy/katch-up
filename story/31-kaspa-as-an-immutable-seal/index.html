Kaspa as an immutable seal

Aug 23, 2026 11:13 UTC 1 min read

What Happened

Community members are discussing a clever way to prove a document existed at a specific time and hasn't been tampered with, without actually uploading the private file to the blockchain. Instead, they use a unique digital fingerprint (or "hash") of the document and record it on the Kaspa network using a simple transaction.

Why It Matters

This matters because it offers a fast, incredibly cheap, and secure way to create permanent digital records and track official document updates over time.
